roofline ceiling — on a memory-bound lane. Two judges built so perf work can
answer "how far from target" instead of "faster than my last version":

  recoverable on the bandwidth axis, ~2% on the compute axis. `attn_qkv` runs
  2.3× slower than the simple R0 probe kernel. Compute-side dot variants
  (direct/pair/row-pair/min-sum) rejected as a class.
  Tool `tools/intel-qwen36-bandwidth-roofline-reject.py`,
  reference `doc/reference/intel-qwen36-35b-a3b-gguf-q4km/bandwidth-roofline-reject-2026-06-29.md`,
  artifact `output/bandwidth-roofline-reject-20260628T161001Z/`.
- **R2 speed-denominator judge.** Native decode ~4.2 tok/s = 0.073× the 1k floor
  (~14× slower than same-host llama.cpp), 0.34% of roofline. Sub-1k smoke; the
  real matrix runs on target. Tool `tools/intel-qwen36-r2-speed-denominator.py`,
  SOP `doc/sop/intel-qwen36-35b-a3b-gguf-q4km/r2-speed-denominator.md`,
