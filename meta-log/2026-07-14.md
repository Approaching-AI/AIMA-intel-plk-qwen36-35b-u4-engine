## Goal and roadmap correction

- The product target remains exact `2k/4k/8k/16k/32k/64k/128k`, output512,
  and `1.10x` same-run stock OpenVINO independently in prefill and decode.
- Work order is now explicitly long-context first. OV2 is active; OV1 adjacent
  fusion, OV3 prefill envelopes, and OV4 exact-bucket promotion are parked in
  that order. Short rows remain hard guards and no phase/bucket averaging is
  allowed.

## Tiled split rejected; single state owner selected

- A bounded dirty-worktree prototype split tiled attention into eager
  prefill/state-write, decode-partial, and finalizer nodes. Diagnostic prefill
  reached candidate/stock `35.986/37.978 ms` at 2k and
  `528.421/583.631 ms` at 8k, proving the scalar carrier's prefill gap is
  structurally removable. Complete decode regressed to `1.458/0.825 ms` at 2k
  and `3.813/3.155`, `3.884/3.036 ms` at 8k; the partial reduction alone was
  near stock, while the finalizer and eager no-op consumed the bound.
- The route fails semantics before performance. All query-visible hot K/V
  written regions remained zero. Decode KLD was `0.047932` at 2k and
  `0.535877` at the first 8k decode step even though greedy IDs matched. Known
  writes at both prefill and finalizer inputs still left `query_state()` zero:
  input mutation across fanned-out custom consumers is not a graph-owned
  OpenVINO Variable transition. The split code was removed; `/tmp/iq36-hot-cold-tiled-confirm-2k8k/`,
  `/tmp/iq36-profile-flat-scalar-confirm-2k8k/`, and
  `/tmp/iq36-prefill-state-probe/` are ephemeral diagnostic evidence, not
  promotion artifacts or speed claims.
- Intel F16 subgroup block reads in the main reduction produced non-finite
  logits and `[271,0,0]`; padded stride/alignment did not repair them. Scalar
  loads restored finite exact paths. Long-hot-only 512-key and 256-key chunk
  split-decode variants also regressed. These bounded forms are closed.
- OV2 gate 2d now requires one request-state owner per layer, preferably one
  custom node covering prefill, decode, and hot/cold transitions. A
  graph-visible output/`Assign` carrier is admissible only behind a complete
  copy bound. Exact 2k/8k state and KLD close first, then split decode overhead,
  the 16k transition, and priority `32k/64k/128k` output512. Exact-bucket short
  candidates may retain stock SDPA.

## Single-owner race and numeric attribution

- Recombining the tiled path into one state-owning custom operation exposed a
  cross-workgroup publication race: repeated candidate attention outputs
  changed with identical inputs, and the last workgroup could consume partial
  workspace before other workgroups' global writes were device-visible.
  OpenCL 3.0 device-scope release/acquire fences around the atomic arrival
  protocol remove the nondeterminism and all-ten NaNs.
- Keeping raw scores and normalization in F32 closes the one-layer arithmetic
  gap. Full prefill attention differs from stock by about `6.7e-6` relative L2
  with almost every F16 element exact; exact 2k/8k one-layer distribution and
  greedy gates pass. Private- and SLM-score all-ten diagnostics are finite and
  exact-greedy but both leave the final 8k KLD at `0.007930`, so all-ten
  semantics remain open. A bounded scalar short-decode replay moved it only to
  `0.007770`; prefill/all-layer accumulation, not the final split reduction
  alone, is the next capture question and that replay was not retained.
- Moving F32 scores from private arrays to SLM recovers some codegen but remains
  diagnostic-only at 2k: candidate/stock attention is `55.468/37.702 ms`
  prefill and `2.780/0.802 ms` decode. The next question is unified-source
  register/ISA coupling, not another tile or arithmetic sweep.
- After commits `7e8f7fb/eb1d559`, clean seq846 passes every one-layer exact
  2k/8k state, codec, distribution, and greedy check. Clean seq847 closes live
  all-ten 2k phase attribution with candidate/stock `58.696/37.564 ms` prefill
  and `2.964/0.831 ms` decode; its carrier-admission result is correctly false.
